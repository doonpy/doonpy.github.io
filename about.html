💻 Poon and Programming

Personally, I wasn't necessarily a programming enthusiast right from the start; it gradually developed through exposure and experience. By now, it has become a passion and an indispensable part of my life (passionate about making money 🤑).

Programming is a long and enduring journey, so passion is something indispensable for every programmer. It would be a hellish experience if you engage in this game without passion or at least interest. Moreover, knowledge is a precious "asset" that programmers continuously "exploit" throughout their journey, as there exists a fun yet profound formula:

Lack of understanding + Enthusiasm = Destruction

Therefore, I relentlessly learn from various sources to enrich my knowledge as much as possible.

If you have hammer everything looks like nail

I'm a Software Engineer based in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam, focused on building scalable full-stack systems, AI-driven products, and cloud-native infrastructure. Over the years I've worked across fintech, healthcare, e-commerce, and AI skincare — from high-frequency trading systems to LLM-powered agents.

🚀 Experience

Building Exa AI, an AI-driven, science-backed skincare platform that uses proprietary algorithms and genetic profiling to diagnose dermatological conditions and deliver personalized, sustainable regimens for 4,000+ users.

  • Architected a scalable full-stack platform (TypeScript, Next.js) and engineered an AI-driven skincare agent leveraging Dify workflows and RAG architectures.
